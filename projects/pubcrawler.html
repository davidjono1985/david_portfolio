Pubcrawler: the ultimate terminal app

A Ruby terminal game, built in a team during my coding bootcamp (2019), that takes the player on a whirlwind tour of pubs and pub culture.

Simple command-line choices have a direct effect on the health and longevity of the player, giving a tongue-in-cheek “night on the town”.

This is historical work, kept as part of my early-work archive.

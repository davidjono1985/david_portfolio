KRAKEN.FM - Music List Builder

My aim was to create a web-tool that can:

  • - allow a user to create a list of songs or import from Spotify account
  • - allow them to choose their favourite songs for each artist
    • - and export to an excel spreadsheet  

The purpose of this tool was to help with building office playlists in order to manually test a business idea.
